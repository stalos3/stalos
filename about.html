About St. Aloysius

We are a mixed day and boarding Catholic-founded school offering Arts and Sciences at O-Level and A-Level.

Our School

Located in Kigwanya-Bataka, 8 miles off Kampala-Masaka Road, we nurture learners with strong values and a supportive environment.

To provide holistic education that nurtures academic excellence, moral integrity, and social responsibility.

Vision

To be a leading institution that nurtures future leaders through innovation and excellence.

Values

Respect, responsibility, curiosity, and compassion guide our school community.

Students learn to lead, collaborate, and serve with confidence.
